Literature head-to-head¶

A path under benchmarks/ on this page names a record of the d-SEAMS 2.0 benchmark campaign. The records and the Snakemake rules that write them are in the private paper repository and the Hugging Face dataset d-SEAMS/dseams2-benchmark-records (docs/records/README.md names the revision); the scripts under scripts/ that time a run stay here.

Cutoff neighbours, local \(q_4\) and \(q_6\), the diamond common-neighbour labels, metal CNA, primitive rings, local ice symmetry, 5^{12} cages and coordination, against mdapy, pyscal3, vesin, IRA, SQQ, ClavisCage and PLUMED.

The statistics are scripts/h2h_protocol.py: one warm-up, eight timed calls in one process, the median of the even sample, and type-7 quartiles. One thread is pinned to cpu 0. Eight threads are pinned to cpus 0-7. The allowed cpu list is recorded. The host name is not. This session saw 8 cpus, 0-7, on an Intel Xeon. d-SEAMS is 2.14.0. The symmetry backend in this build is the in-tree Kabsch kernel; libira is not linked.

IRA is the conda-forge package ira (import ira_mod). The PyPI package also named ira is version 0.0 and has no ira_mod, so it is not the shape-matching library. SQQ 0.5.8 installs from PyPI. ClavisCage is the package clavis-engine (import clavis_engine). PLUMED is the 2.10 symfunc build named by PLUMED_KERNEL. A build without that module has no Q4, Q6 or COORDINATIONNUMBER.

Frames come from scripts/gen_ideal_ice.py and are not stored. The oxygen bond is 2.75 Å and the cutoff is 3.5 Å. Ice Ic is the 8-atom cubic cell. Ice Ih is the 8-atom orthorhombic cell made from two hexagonal primitives, so the box has no tilt. The molecule counts are 128, 4096 and 110592. Two frames already in the tree are included. input/traj/dump-l.lammpstrj frame 1 is 512 atoms of type 1. Its number density is 0.311 Å^{-3} and the median number of neighbours inside 3.5 Å is 56, so it is not liquid water; the domain test calls it the liquid frame. input/traj/exampleTraj.lammpstrj contributes its 250 type-2 oxygens, the mixed TIP4P example.

Agreement is per atom. Neighbour rows are the same sets. \(q_l\) agrees when every atom is within \(10^{-6}\). Diamond labels are compared after mapping d-SEAMS cubic (1) and hexagonal (0) onto the mdapy and pyscal3 codes 1 and 4. Metal CNA is a different label set and is reported against the diamond label so the mismatch is visible. Rings agree when the size census matches. Local symmetry agrees when the IRA template with the smaller Hausdorff distance is the same ice label, and a match worse than 0.2 Å is left unclassified.

snakemake literature_h2h regenerates benchmarks/h2h-literature.csv and the raw text log benchmarks/h2h-literature.txt. Set H2H_PYTHON to an interpreter with the five packages, and SEAMS_CORE_LIB to the release libyodaLib.so. benchmarks/h2h_literature.py is the one-call Airspeed Velocity arm of the same commands, one class for each code, at one thread and at eight.

**Neighbours.** The cutoff list matches vesin, mdapy and pyscal3 on every frame (agreement 1). On ice Ic of 110592 molecules the medians are 12.958 ms for d-SEAMS, 48.884 ms for vesin, 42.080 ms for mdapy and 175.153 ms for pyscal3, at one thread. At eight threads the same list is 4.287 ms against vesin at 50.681 ms, mdapy at 62.605 ms and pyscal3 at 185.708 ms. Eight threads are under 20 ms and faster than one thread. Ice Ih of the same size is 25.773 ms against vesin at 47.958 ms. The 128-molecule cells and the 512-atom dense frame used to miss the one-cell stencil, because that stencil required four cells on every edge. Two cells are enough: the other image is at least half an edge away, and half an edge is at least the cutoff, so the strict test keeps a single image. After that change the 128-molecule ice Ic list is 0.059 ms against vesin at 0.095 ms, and the dense frame is 1.384 ms against mdapy at 1.635 ms and vesin at 1.992 ms, still with the same neighbour sets.

q4 and q6. Local Steinhardt parameters \(q_4\) and \(q_6\) on the cutoff list. On ideal ice the first shell is a perfect tetrahedron, so Ih and Ic share the same \(q_l\). Every compared value is within \(10^{-6}\); the largest absolute gap on the 110592-molecule cells is a few times \(10^{-16}\). At one thread on ice Ic of that size, \(q_4\) is 17.536 ms against mdapy 59.907 ms and pyscal3 86.938 ms, and \(q_6\) is 18.196 ms against mdapy 73.259 ms and pyscal3 97.298 ms. At eight threads \(q_4\) is 5.049 ms against mdapy 80.176 ms and pyscal3 90.943 ms, and \(q_6\) is 5.240 ms against mdapy 94.818 ms and pyscal3 101.745 ms.

**Diamond and metal CNA.** Diamond labels agree on the ideal cells: every ice Ic molecule is cubic, every ice Ih molecule is hexagonal. They also agree on the dense frame and on the TIP4P oxygens: every atom is other. Metal CNA (fcc, hcp, bcc, icosahedral) labels every ice molecule other. That is the correct CNA answer. Those signatures are not ice classes. The diamond common-neighbour label is the one that names cubic and hexagonal ice, and it matches. On the dense frame the 3.5 Å shell holds about 56 neighbours. mdapy’s diamond identifier uses the four nearest, and that list produces the same labels as the cutoff list: every atom is other. d-SEAMS takes those four nearest when a uniform ball of the cutoff would hold more than 12 neighbours, and leaves a crystal shell on the cutoff list. The dense-frame labels match the cutoff-list labels. The ideal cells are unchanged. At one thread the dense frame is 0.411 ms against mdapy at 0.576 ms. At eight threads it is 0.310 ms against mdapy at 10.356 ms.

**Rings.** Primitive rings of size at most 6. On ice Ic, and on ice Ih at 4096 and 110592 molecules, the census is two six-rings per molecule and no smaller ring. d-SEAMS, mdapy and SQQ agree. At 110592 molecules of ice Ic and one thread the medians are 91.763 ms, mdapy 2187.852 ms and SQQ 15248.409 ms. At eight threads d-SEAMS is 28.945 ms, mdapy is 1640.954 ms and SQQ is 13893.758 ms. The census is 221184 six-rings. SQQ does not search three-rings, and one frame does not split across its workers, so eight threads do not change its time. The 128-molecule ice Ih cell is the exception. d-SEAMS and SQQ both report 32 four-rings and 384 six-rings. mdapy reports 0 and 256. Those 32 four-rings are chordless cycles of the bond graph: the opposite vertices are graph distance 2 and there is no chord. They are primitive. They close through the periodic boundary of the 2 by 2 by 4 orthorhombic cell. At 4096 molecules and above the extra cycles are gone and the three codes agree. The d-SEAMS census is the more complete primitive count on the small cell. The dense frame has median coordination 56, and the primitive search returns 125065 three-rings, 118033 four-rings, 2986554 five-rings and 945837 six-rings (12998 ms at one thread). mdapy and SQQ were not asked to repeat that search.

**IRA.** Each atom’s 16 nearest neighbours inside 5 Å, plus the atom itself, are matched to the ice Ih and ice Ic templates. The template with the smaller Hausdorff distance is the label, unless that distance exceeds 0.2 Å. On the ideal cells the chosen distance is 0 and every label matches seams_symmetry_classify. At 110592 molecules of ice Ic the classify median is 24.319 ms at one thread and 6.075 ms at eight. IRA is 26568.831 ms and 29043.763 ms. Eight threads do not make IRA faster. The eight-core IRA time does not fall, because the matcher is one permutation search per shell. Both answers are the same ice label. The d-SEAMS number is the bond-graph kernel, not a call into libira. IRA remains the right tool for a cluster that is not one of these ice shells.

**Cages.** ClavisCage 0.3.18 runs TRACE, the hydrogen-bond cage search. Hydrogens sit 0.96 Å along the two nearest oxygen-oxygen bonds. d-SEAMS times the 5^{12} count from seams_frame_signature and reads the 5^{12}6^{2} and 5^{12}6^{4} counts once on the same graph. On ice and on the TIP4P oxygens the 3.5 Å list is that graph, and TRACE uses its default 0.35 nm cutoff and 35 degree angle. The dense frame’s 0.35 nm shell has median degree 56, and TRACE does not return on it. Both codes then use the four nearest neighbours: the mutual graph, and a TRACE cutoff just past the fourth neighbour at 1.413 Å. Every frame reports no cage of any of the three kinds. Ice has no five-rings, so a dodecahedron is absent. At 110592 molecules of ice Ic the timed count is 59.557 ms at one thread and 15.924 ms at eight, against TRACE at 368.404 ms and 367.804 ms. Both counts are zero cages. Eight threads do not make TRACE faster. Ice Ih is 59.631 ms and 38.656 ms against 260.930 ms and 259.011 ms.

**PLUMED.** The rows in this section are the previous sitting. conda-forge plumed 2.10.2 does not build the symfunc module, so Q4 and Q6 are absent from that kernel. The release candidate sitting further down builds PLUMED 2.10.2 with every module, including symfunc, and remeasures both orders.

Q4, Q6 and COORDINATIONNUMBER. The neighbour weight is a steep rational switch cut at 3.5 Å. PLUMED stores the norm of the averaged spherical-harmonic vector and omits \(\sqrt{4\pi/(2l+1)}\). Restoring that factor makes it the local Steinhardt parameter. On ideal ice every restored value is within \(10^{-6}\) of d-SEAMS, and the coordination number is 4. The PLUMED rows in that sitting were measured on one thread because PLUMED_NUM_THREADS was unset. On ice Ic of 110592 molecules the recorded \(q_4\) is 57.042 ms against PLUMED 1294.172 ms and \(q_6\) is 48.591 ms against 1671.743 ms. The sharp neighbour count is 31.356 ms against 161.853 ms. The columns the driver labeled eight threads, near 1304 ms, 1692 ms and 163 ms, are that same one-thread measurement. The 884736-molecule sitting sets PLUMED_NUM_THREADS, so its q6 rows are not this measurement. On the dense frame and the TIP4P slab some neighbours sit on the cutoff, so the continuous switch is a different number. The largest \(q_4\) gap on the dense frame is 0.025 and the coordination gap is 6.8. The sharp list is the value that matches mdapy, pyscal3 and vesin. The dense-frame \(q_4\) median is 2.217 ms against PLUMED 76.950 ms.

The raw lines, including every sample, are benchmarks/h2h-literature.txt. The same run writes benchmarks/h2h-literature.png from the medians. The image is not stored in git.

Release candidate, one sitting¶

scripts/h2h_rc3_sitting.py is the run. snakemake rc3_sitting regenerates it. benchmarks/h2h_watch.py class Rc3Sitting is one call of the same worker. One warm-up, then six timed calls. The median of the even sample is the mean of the two central values. The interquartile range is type 7. Eight cores, Intel Xeon family 6 model 207, one thread per core, under KVM. The host name is not recorded. d-SEAMS is 2.14.0, built at -O3. vesin is 0.6.2, mdapy is 2.0.0, freud is 3.6.1, OVITO is 3.17.2, PLUMED is 2.10.2 with the symfunc module, rings-statistics-matter is 0.3.0, ClavisCage is 0.3.18 and fastatomstruct is 2.2.0.

Ideal ice Ic, oxygen bond 2.75 Å, cutoff 3.5 Å. The q4 mean is 0.50917508 and the q6 mean is 0.62853936. Six-rings are two per molecule. CHILL+ calls every molecule cubic. OVITO’s polyhedral template match, with the two diamond templates enabled, labels every molecule, and the shell symmetry kernel does the same on this crystal.

A one-thread rival is pinned to cpu 0. Eight threads use cpus 0-7. Neighbour, CHILL+, ring and cage calls widen the process mask to every cpu the kernel still allows, so those one-thread rows record cores 0-7. The OpenMP width on those rows is still the requested one thread. q4 and q6 do not widen the mask.

**Cages.** The frames are GenIce, not ice. Hydrogens are an ice-rule orientation of the four nearest oxygens, built before the timer. TRACE drops a ring that crosses the periodic boundary. On the sI unit cell (46 oxygens) d-SEAMS finds 2 of 5^{12} and 6 of 5^{12}6^{2}, and TRACE finds 1 of 5^{12}. The 2×2×2 replicate (368 oxygens) is the same crystal with the cages inside the cell: both codes find 16 and 48. On sII (136 oxygens) both find 16 of 5^{12} and 8 of 5^{12}6^{4}.

**Where d-SEAMS is slower.** On no paired row. The PLUMED q4 and q6 rows were measured on one thread because PLUMED_NUM_THREADS was unset, so the threads column is the width the driver requested. The 884736-molecule sitting sets the variable. TRACE does not change from one thread to eight. The sI unit-cell census is the disagreement above. The q6 derivative, checked separately on a symmetry-broken eight-molecule cell, matches an independent nanometer difference to 3.99e-11.
