Head-to-head¶

Measured comparisons of d-SEAMS 2.14.0 with other structure codes. The cage census is read out of the agreement text of the cage comparison.

A time in the summary and cage tables is the median of n repeats. The ratio is the other median divided by the d-SEAMS median. In those two tables every stored ratio is greater than 1: the other median is the larger time. Where a table has an agreement column, that column is the stored agreement text through the first semicolon. The cage timing table’s last column is the cage name. The census under it is the rest of the agreement text. Notes about serial calls are in the section prose.

The three comparisons¶

In the summary and cage tables, the version column is the version field. A slash, when present, separates the d-SEAMS version from the other code. Every summary row records cores 8. A one-thread summary row records cpus 0. An eight-thread summary row records cpus 0-7.

In the SQQ comparison, each timing row records d-SEAMS 2.14.0 and SQQ 0.5.8. Those rows store means. The summary tables store medians. The SQQ ratio is ratio_sqq_over_dseams.

The 884736-molecule comparison of ice and a TIP4P/ice liquid is on Performance. On the TIP4P/ice liquid the table states a freud \(q_6\) gap of 1.525e-04, called hard-cutoff rounding there, and a PLUMED \(q_6\) gap of 1.679e-01, also written 0.168, called the rational switch there. CHILL+ agrees on 830736 of 884736 molecules. The \(q_6\) table on this page is the summary comparison. Its agreement column is the maximum absolute gap stored for that size.

CHILL+¶

mdapy 2.0.0 matches the label on every molecule and stores the same q6_max_abs as its q6 rows. ClavisCage 0.3.18 matches the label on every molecule. The agreement text on the ClavisCage rows states that this CHILL+ path is a serial loop. The table is the measured time: the eight-thread ClavisCage median stays with the one-thread median at each size.

pyscal3 4.1.0 has no finished CHILL+ row. The agreement text says that version exports no CHILL or CHILL+ name, that common_neighbor_analysis is adaptive CNA and returns fcc, hcp, bcc, ico, and other, and that diamond_structure is extended CNA. The same text says neither function is the Nguyen and Molinero CHILL+ rule, and that PyPI’s latest pyscal3 is this same 4.1.0. The rows are recorded at one thread and at eight, with an empty molecule count.

Rings¶

OVITO 3.17.2 FindRings, rings-statistics-matter 0.3.0, and cycless 0.7, on the rows that finished. The agreement text is the matching hexagon count: 8192, 221184, and 1769472 at 4096, 110592, and 884736 molecules.

The cycless rows state that cycles_iter has no thread argument. At 884736 molecules the cycless rows did not finish. The version field on those rows is 0.7, one sample exceeded 600 s, and the agreement text stores a fit labelled “Fit, not a measurement.” There is no measured time for that size.

freud has no ring row. The agreement text names freud 3.6.1, lists its modules, and states that there is no ring or cycle module. The citation in that text is Ramasubramani and others, Comput. Phys. Commun. 254, 107275 (2020). The version field on those rows is 2.14.0. The sizes are 4096, 110592, and 884736, at one thread and at eight.

Per-atom q4 and q6¶

Per-atom \(q_4\) and \(q_6\), the Lechner-Dellago averages, and the mean of each, are compared on three frames. Ideal ice Ih is input/traj/ice1h.lammpstrj. Ideal ice Ic is a diamond cell with a 2.75 Å bond, four cells on a side. The liquid is that cell plus Gaussian noise of 0.9 Å, seed 91001. The neighbour rule is the same in d-SEAMS, freud, and pyscal3: a pair counts when its minimum-image distance is strictly below 3.5 Å. The comparison runs at one thread and at eight.

pyscal3 is float64. Local \(q_l\), the Lechner-Dellago average, and the mean of each over atoms that have a shell agree to \(10^{-10}\). freud stores \(q_l\) as float32. On the two crystals the gap stays within 8 units in the last place of that float32 value. On the liquid the spherical-harmonic sum is accumulated in float32, and the residual stays under \(2 \times 10^{-5}\).

An empty shell is 0 in d-SEAMS and NaN in freud and pyscal3. 0 is the empty accumulator. Local q4 and q6 records why that 0 stays: a NaN would enter every mean and every CHILL+ label.

OVITO 3.15.5 has no SteinhardtParametersModifier. OVITO 3.17.2 \(q_4\) and \(q_6\) are timed in the 884736-molecule comparison on Performance.
