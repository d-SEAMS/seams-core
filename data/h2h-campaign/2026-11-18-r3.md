## what the timed call covers

- thread controls: every sample process is pinned to one CPU core via taskset with single-thread env vars (OMP, MKL, OPENBLAS, NUMBA, TBB, RAYON, POLARS, TOKIO, NUMEXPR, OVITO set to 1) and the tool's 1-thread API knob.
- threads: observed OS threads in /proc/self/status after the timed call: 1392/2472 good samples had 1 OS thread; 1080/2472 good samples had >1 OS thread.
- multi-thread tools (os_threads > 1 despite 1-thread controls and taskset): fastatomstruct (3), mdapy (3), ovito (2), rings-statistics-matter (9), vesin (8).
- cycless/rings: takes a graph, not positions: the 3.5 A graph is built before the timer
- ovito/rings: CreateBonds then FindRings in one pipeline; the table has no 3-rings
- sqq/rings: reports no 3-rings
- seams/knn: undirected union of the 4-nearest-neighbour relations, rows may exceed 4
