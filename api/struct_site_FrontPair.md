## Detailed Documentation {#detailed-documentation}

Two planar fronts of one ice slab in a periodic box.

`zEnter` is where the ice fraction crosses `cut` on entering the ice along +z, and `zExit` is the crossing on leaving it. `thickness` is the ice run along z.
