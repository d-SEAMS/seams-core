# Code of Conduct

This package follows the [Contributor Covenant 1.4](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/1/4/code-of-conduct.html)
used by seams-core. The full text is
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](https://github.com/d-SEAMS/seams-core/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
in that repository.

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our
project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project team leader at
[rgoswami@ieee.org](mailto:rgoswami@ieee.org). Reports are reviewed and
kept confidential.

Participants in rOpenSci review also follow
[rOpenSci's Code of Conduct](https://ropensci.org/code-of-conduct/).
