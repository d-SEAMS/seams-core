Performance, 4096 to 110592¶

Neighbour lists and primitive rings from the comparisons other than the release-candidate sitting and the 884736-molecule head-to-head. A row is listed when its stored n is at least 6. The interval is q1 ms and q3 ms when recorded, iqr ms when that is the recorded interval, and min ms when that is the second time recorded. The version column is the version the row recorded. Two of the tables store a variant column, rings or bonds_and_rings.

One cycless comparison also stores a 1000-molecule row. It is listed with that table. Rows whose stored n is below 6 are omitted.

The release-candidate medians are on the sitting page. The 884736-molecule ice and liquid medians are on the head-to-head. What each comparison measures is on the overview.
