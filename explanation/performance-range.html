Performance, 4096 to 110592¶

Neighbour lists and primitive rings against freud, OVITO, mdapy, pyscal3, vesin, IRA, PTM, cycless, and every other code that has a neighbour or ring row in benchmarks/*.csv. IRA and PTM have no neighbour or ring row in those files. The molecule field is molecules. The interval is q1_ms and q3_ms when the file stores them, and iqr_ms when that is the interval it stores. n is the sample count in the file. A row with no time, or a verdict of absent or DNF, is written DNF.

The classifier, polyhedral template matching, and IRA rows are benchmarks/paper-numbers/sitting-large.csv. ratio_vs_dseams is the ratio of medians. ratio_lo and ratio_hi are the interval stored in that file. source_commit is the commit column.

The tables are rendered from those files in the benchmark records dataset (docs/records/README.md names the revision).
