`src/include/external` is gone. `cxxopts.hpp`, `backward.hpp` and `rang.hpp` are now Meson WrapDB wraps (`subprojects/cxxopts.wrap`, `subprojects/backward-cpp.wrap`, `subprojects/rang.wrap`) picked up through `dependency(..., fallback: ...)`, so a system package satisfies them too. `argum.h`, the CLI's locally patched argument parser, moved to `third_party/argum/` (see its `NOTICE.md` for the patch and why it is not wrapped); `iralib_interf.h` moved to `src/include/internal/`. A new `with_backward` auto feature controls the pretty-stack-trace build, off in the packaging recipes that cannot reach the network.
