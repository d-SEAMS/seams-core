Add `pydseams.TrajectorySequence`, a multi-frame trajectory container opened with `pydseams.open_trajectory()`, with lazy streaming, `len`, negative indexing, slicing, unwrapping, and the MSD, hydrogen-bond, and reorientation accumulators. `pydseams.Trajectory` stays an alias of the single-frame `pydseams.Frame` throughout 2.14.x, so `Trajectory is Frame` still holds; a future major version may rebind `Trajectory` to the sequence class.
