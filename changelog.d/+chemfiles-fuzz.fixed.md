Every format the chemfiles build enables was fuzzed under AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer, and each crash, hang and sanitizer report is fixed in its reader. A truncated AMBER7 parm or parm7 file, a Gaussian cube with a huge atom count, an mmCIF row without its model column, a one-byte Maestro file, a PQR line that starts with NUL, a BINPOS atom count that overflows, and hostile DESRES, js, XSF, XBGF, CAR, DL_POLY, g96, TRJ, XTC, TRR, TPR, DCD, Amber NetCDF, CSSR, GRO, SMI, TNG and MMTF files are errors. A VTF reader that runs out of memory keeps its bond count, and the molfile and LAMMPS Data readers define `S_ISREG` where the C library lacks it (MSVC). Each fix has a fixture under `input/hostile/` that `chemfiles_hostile` reads.
