Each engine runs for 200 steps on ice and liquid (mW in LAMMPS and in LAMMPS with `fix plumed`, TIP4P/Ice in GROMACS, in `gmx mdrun -plumed` and in OpenMM), dumps the coordinates d-SEAMS classified with their labels, and requires `seams chill-plus` to give the same label to every site of every frame. The reproducibility recipe writes the measurements to `data/engines-e2e.csv`.
