Smooth collective variables with analytic gradients for biased sampling and Markov state models. `seams_switch_rational()` is the PLUMED RATIONAL switch with a C2 taper to zero at d_max. A `seams_cv` handle fixes the candidate rings between neighbour-list rebuilds and returns soft primitive-ring counts by size, ring-atom counts, soft HC basal hexagon pairs, the gyration radius and ellipsoid volume of a cluster, and products of logistic state clauses, each with its gradient in the coordinates under periodic boundaries.
