Directional bin volumes past a nonzero origin sum to the cell volume. A wedge of half a turn is split so two azimuthal bins have a real sector volume. A radial shell whose edge lands on a face vertex keeps the circular sector. Host-guest distances use the triclinic minimum image. The radial coordinate beyond the inscribed radius is the distance to the nearest periodic image of the pore axis, and the shell volume is the corresponding Voronoi cell. Mass density is the caller's mass unit per cubic Angstrom.
