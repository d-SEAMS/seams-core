The chemfiles that d-SEAMS builds reads every VMD molfile format chemfiles 0.10.4 can compile and that returns atoms: 33 plugins and 37 formats instead of 3. Among them are PQR, XSF, Gaussian cube, AMBER7 restart and parm7, CHARMM coordinates, Maestro, VTF, DESRES trajectories and DMS, Gromacs g96, CPMD and Gaussian logs, DL_POLY, GAMESS, and VASP POSCAR, OUTCAR, XDATCAR and vasprun.xml. `cmake/patches/chemfiles-0.10.4-molfiles.patch` enables them in the Meson and CMake builds, and the DMS reader links the system SQLite3. Counting the frames of an AMBER7 restart no longer crashes, an AMBER7 restart with no atom count on its second line no longer hangs, and an empty file or a file in which a plugin finds no atoms is an error.
