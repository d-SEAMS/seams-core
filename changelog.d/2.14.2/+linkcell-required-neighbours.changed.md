linkcell is the only neighbour backend and is always built; it needs linkcell 0.6.0, and `subprojects/linkcell.wrap` pins the v0.6.0 tag, the first release with `lc_pairs_within_csr`. Every cutoff list (`neighListO`, `halfNeighList`, `getNewNeighbourListByIndex`, `neighListPair`, the refill variants, `reusedShellList` and the `SkinNeighborList` candidates) comes from that threaded CSR query on orthorhombic, triclinic and open boundaries, and k-nearest lists come from linkcell alone. The vesin path, the in-tree cell lists, the in-tree k-nearest graph and the brute-force loops are removed, so `-Dwith_linkcell=disabled` is a configure error and the option is deprecated. The rows are the bytes the removed builders gave. A cutoff that is not finite and positive throws `std::invalid_argument`, a coordinate that is not finite throws `linkcell::Error`, a `SkinNeighborList` without a box throws, and the CHILL+ C entry points return nonzero with the message in `seams_c_error()` instead of letting the exception out.
