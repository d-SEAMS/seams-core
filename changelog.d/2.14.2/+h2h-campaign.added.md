The head-to-head campaign times d-SEAMS against freud, OVITO, vesin, mdapy, pyscal3, rings-statistics-matter, SQQ, fastatomstruct and cycless for neighbour lists (a 3.5 A cutoff and the four nearest neighbours) and ring statistics on ice, mixed-phase, mW water and sII hydrate frames from 136 to 110592 molecules. `scripts/h2h_campaign.py` takes one sample per fresh process pinned to one required `--core` with single-thread environment variables and tool knobs and no warm-up call, interleaves the arms and the cells round by round with the odd rounds reversed, runs d-SEAMS twice in every cell as its own A/A band, records wall time, CPU time, peak resident memory, `os_threads`, and `calls_per_sample`, and checks round-0 neighbour-list outputs against d-SEAMS and (for `cutoff` and `knn` on frames up to 4096 molecules) against a brute-force minimum-image oracle; ring statistics (`rings`, because the oracle covers only quadratic minimum-image neighbour sets) and frames above 4096 molecules (`ice32768` and `ice110592`, due to quadratic runtime and memory cost) are excluded from the brute-force oracle and cross-checked against d-SEAMS. Its `lanes` subcommand runs the asv timing lanes with `SEAMS_BENCH_PREWARM=0` and records per-sample call and warm-up counts on every row, and the `campaign_h2h` suite exposes only valid head-to-head cells under asv. The record `data/h2h-campaign/<stamp>.jsonl` is append-only and carries the host id, pinned core, CPU model, lscpu summary, kernel, code revision, upstream base revision and commit tree on every row, and reports the actual observed OS thread counts from the record.
