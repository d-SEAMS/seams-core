A linked-cell search that cannot represent the frame's cell, because an edge needs more bins than linkcell allows or the cell is too large for its cell construction, is `SEAMS_ERR_RANGE` from the C API with linkcell's message, instead of `SEAMS_ERR_INTERNAL`. The mutual neighbour graph and the other k-nearest paths on a needle cell, such as edges 2^30, 1 and 0.375, report it that way. A non-finite coordinate reported by linkcell is `SEAMS_ERR_ARG`.
