An exception inside a threaded neighbour, contact, CHILL, CHILL+, Steinhardt, bond-order, cage-matching, crystal-template or SOAP loop that measures minimum-image distances is returned to the caller once the loop ends, at one thread, at many threads and without OpenMP, instead of terminating the process. `seams_contact_pairs` on a cell outside the minimum-image range returns `SEAMS_ERR_RANGE` instead of aborting.
