Under mpiexec, an MPI build of `seams` splits a LAMMPS dump's `--frame`/`--last` range into contiguous blocks, one per rank, and rank 0 prints every frame in order; other readers stay on rank 0, `--per-atom` needs a single rank, and a run without a launcher never starts MPI. `chill::setSteinhardtAtomSplit(false)` keeps `steinhardtQl` from splitting atoms over ranks that hold different frames. On 32 frames of 32768-atom ice, `cages` on eight ranks keeps a parallel efficiency of 0.99 between `MPI_Init` and `MPI_Finalize` (load balance 0.99, communication efficiency 1.00) and takes 5.3 s against 32.2 s serially; on one node eight `--jobs` workers take 4.28 s, since Open MPI's launch costs about a second (release builds).
