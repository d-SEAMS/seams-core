Fractional density profiles follow a triclinic cell axis or an arbitrary direction, with slab volumes taken from the cell. Pore profiles add a cell-edge axis and centre, cylindrical radial and azimuthal shells, and axial slabs, as number and mass density. A radial shell is the Voronoi cell of the perpendicular lattice clipped by the outer radius, times the pore-edge length. A crossing of that circle with a polygon edge is kept on the edge, including a crossing that lands on a vertex. Azimuthal sectors split a wedge, including a wedge of half a turn. Azimuth zero points along the perpendicular projection of the next cell edge, (axis + 1) mod 3. The radial coordinate is the distance to the nearest periodic image of the axis. Axis -1 selects the longest cell edge, and any other negative axis is rejected. r_max of zero covers the perpendicular face, and a negative r_max is rejected.

Host-guest queries report the nearest framework site and contact counts by framework type. The distance is the triclinic minimum image. The shared helper is triclinicMinImage, also used by site distances, water selection, and the periodic distance functions, with the same refinement applied to batched, cached-frame, and 2D RDF (`rdf2::sampleRDF_AA` / `seams_frame_rdf2d`) distances outside the Smith ball. Outside the Smith ball it refines both the fractional wrap and, when linked, minimage's candidate. The cell is reduced by Lagrange and Selling steps, and each reduced vector is re-evaluated from its integer coordinates in the cell with an error-free dot product. From the Babai point, a descent then runs over the shortest member of each coset of L / 2L, which contains every Voronoi-relevant vector, so the result is the exact minimum image on every LAMMPS-legal cell. That includes FCC primitive, tiny tilts, tilts at `xy = lx/2` and at the corner `|xy| = |xz| = lx/2`, `|yz| = ly/2`, and elongated cells. A step that cannot lower the binary64 norm ends the descent instead of raising an error. Non-finite inputs or intermediates, singular bases, and lattice coefficients at or above 2^52 report an error without poisoning the per-thread basis cache. The returned length is within about ten binary64 ulps of the wrapped displacement's length. On random legal cells the worst relative error is `2e-15` up to an edge aspect ratio of 40, `1e-14` up to `1e3`, `2e-13` up to `1e5`, and `2.4e-11` up to `1e8`, where the wrap can be far longer than the image. A positive three-length box wraps each orthorhombic axis. A box with fewer than six values does not read tilt factors. A guest type equal to the framework type is an error. framework_type 0 keeps every other type.

Mass density is the caller's mass unit per cubic Angstrom. A mass in amu is amu per cubic Angstrom, and multiplying by 1.66054 converts that density to grams per cubic centimetre. The library does not apply the factor.
