Fractional density profiles follow a triclinic cell axis or an arbitrary direction, with slab volumes taken from the cell. Pore profiles add a cell-edge axis and centre, cylindrical radial and azimuthal shells, and axial slabs, as number and mass density. A radial shell is the Voronoi cell of the perpendicular lattice clipped by the outer radius, times the pore-edge length. A crossing of that circle with a polygon edge is kept on the edge, including a crossing that lands on a vertex. Azimuthal sectors split a wedge, including a wedge of half a turn. Azimuth zero points along the perpendicular projection of the next cell edge, (axis + 1) mod 3. The radial coordinate is the distance to the nearest periodic image of the axis. Axis -1 selects the longest cell edge, and any other negative axis is rejected. r_max of zero covers the perpendicular face, and a negative r_max is rejected.

Host-guest queries report the nearest framework site and contact counts by framework type. The distance is the triclinic minimum image. The shared helper is triclinicMinImage, also used by site distances, water selection, and the periodic distance functions, with the same refinement applied to batched, cached-frame, and 2D RDF (`rdf2::sampleRDF_AA` / `seams_frame_rdf2d`) distances outside the Smith ball. Outside the Smith ball it refines both the fractional wrap and, when linked, minimage's candidate by Babai rounding in an uncapped Selling search, skipping non-decreasing Lagrange ties, treating dot products within binary64 rounding as obtuse (with a 26-neighbour and superbasis-difference follow-up when not strictly obtuse), and undoing any Selling step that cannot lower the norm sum. Non-finite inputs or intermediates, singular bases, lattice coefficients at or above 2^52, and unresolved slicer stalls report an error without poisoning the per-thread basis cache. Ordinary triclinic cells including FCC primitive, tiny tilts, and `xy = lx/2` succeed; at extreme aspect ratios (`lx/ly >= ~8e4`), binary64 basis-update rounding can reach `1e-12` to `5.4e-9` relative and a few `~1e8`-aspect cells throw explicitly from the slicer. A positive three-length box wraps each orthorhombic axis. A box with fewer than six values does not read tilt factors. A guest type equal to the framework type is an error. framework_type 0 keeps every other type.

Mass density is the caller's mass unit per cubic Angstrom. A mass in amu is amu per cubic Angstrom, and multiplying by 1.66054 converts that density to grams per cubic centimetre. The library does not apply the factor.
