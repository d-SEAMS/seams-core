Fractional density profiles follow a triclinic cell axis or an arbitrary direction, with slab volumes taken from the cell. Pore profiles add a cell-edge axis and centre, cylindrical radial and azimuthal shells, and axial slabs, as number and mass density. A radial shell is the Voronoi cell of the perpendicular lattice clipped by the outer radius, times the pore-edge length. A crossing of that circle with a polygon edge is kept on the edge, including a crossing that lands on a vertex. Azimuthal sectors split a wedge, including a wedge of half a turn. Azimuth zero points along the perpendicular projection of the next cell edge, (axis + 1) mod 3. The radial coordinate is the distance to the nearest periodic image of the axis. Axis -1 selects the longest cell edge, and any other negative axis is rejected. r_max of zero covers the perpendicular face, and a negative r_max is rejected.

Host-guest queries report the nearest framework site and contact counts by framework type. The distance is the triclinic minimum image. The shared helper is triclinicMinImage, also used by site distances, water selection, and the periodic distance functions. Outside the Smith ball it uses minimage when that library is linked and the uncapped Selling minimum image otherwise, so a build without the optional dependency still returns the Euclidean minimum image, including on a long tilted cell. A positive three-length box wraps each orthorhombic axis. A box with fewer than six values does not read tilt factors. A guest type equal to the framework type is an error. framework_type 0 keeps every other type.

Mass density is the caller's mass unit per cubic Angstrom. A mass in amu is amu per cubic Angstrom, and multiplying by 1.66054 converts that density to grams per cubic centimetre. The library does not apply the factor.
