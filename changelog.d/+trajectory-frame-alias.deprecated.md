Deprecate using `pydseams.Trajectory` as an alias of single-frame `pydseams.Frame`. `Trajectory(path, ...)` and the other single-frame constructor calls still return a `Frame`, with every `Frame` method, and emit `DeprecationWarning`; `Frame` attributes read from a `Trajectory` sequence warn and delegate to its first frame, except `load_frame`, which raises after the warning. `Trajectory is Frame` no longer holds; open a sequence with `Trajectory.open`.
