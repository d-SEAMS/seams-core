Benchmarks¶

Timing tables of the d-SEAMS 2.0 paper and its supplement. Each table names its record, a directory or file under results/reference/ of dseams2_repro. The values below are those of seams-core v2.12.0, the release the paper describes. A rerun at a later release adds its own table under the same heading and names its record, so the paper’s values stay readable next to the current ones. Reproduce gives the commands, and Versions gives the machines and the codes.

The frames are the 4096-molecule mW cubic frame, the final frame of the 2880-water Niu dump, the 1000-molecule continuous random network CRN1 and the 2 x 2 x 2 GenIce sI framework of 368 waters. Every code gets the same oxygen coordinates and orthorhombic cell, and every library runs on one thread.

OVITO CHILL+ and the d-SEAMS CHILL+:math:(r_c) both run at \(r_c=3.5\) Å. d-SEAMS clathrate, interfacial clathrate and water match OVITO hydrate, interfacial hydrate and other. The CHILL+ labels agree on every mW molecule and differ at 270 Niu molecules, 109 of which d-SEAMS labels interfacial clathrate and OVITO other. freud’s timer holds two neighbour searches, since the plain and averaged parameters are two objects, and freud stores coordinates in single precision. d-SEAMS evaluates \(q_6\) on the mutual four-nearest graph and freud on the four nearest neighbours; the two graphs differ at 175 of the 2880 Niu molecules. Given freud’s neighbour rows, the engine returns freud’s values to 3.0e-5. R.I.N.G.S. puts 97% of each run in the distance matrix of the 5 x 5 x 5 superlattice it builds for a periodic cell.

The calls on each side are these.

  • d-SEAMS CHILL+:math:(r_c): neighListO, getCorrelPlus and getIceTypePlusNoPrint through PydSEAMSlib on the 3.5 Å cutoff list

  • d-SEAMS \(q_6\) and \(\bar q_6\): kNearestNeighbourList and steinhardtQl

  • d-SEAMS rings: neighListO, neighbourListByIndex and ringNetwork, with findBySignature added for the sI cages

  • d-SEAMS against PTM: the completed seeded rule of scripts/sota_compare.py

  • d-SEAMS against R.I.N.G.S.: seams fingerprint crn.lammpstrj --cutoff 3.5 --type 1

  • OVITO CHILL+: ChillPlusModifier(cutoff=3.5) on a DataCollection

  • OVITO PTM: PolyhedralTemplateMatchingModifier

  • freud: freud.order.Steinhardt(l=6) with average=False and with average=True, each over four nearest neighbours

  • GenIce2 rings: pairlist.pairs_iter at 3.5 Å into a networkx graph, then cycles_iter(graph, 6, pos) of cycless.cycles

  • GenIce2 cages: those rings, then polyhedra_iter(rings, 14) of cycless.polyhed, keeping the cages of 12 and 14 five- and six-rings, as the genice2-cage formatter does

  • R.I.N.G.S.: rings input on the same coordinates with one species, periodic boundaries, primitive rings only, search depth 3 (rings of up to six members) and the O-O bond at 3.5 Å

The genice2-cage formatter run from the command line first replicates the sI unit cell and builds a hydrogen-bond graph, so the record runs it once, untimed, and it returns the same 16 and 48 cages.
