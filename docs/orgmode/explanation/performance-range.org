#+TITLE: Performance, 4096 to 110592
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

Neighbour lists and primitive rings against freud, OVITO, mdapy,
pyscal3, vesin, IRA, PTM, cycless, and every other code that has a
neighbour or ring result. IRA and PTM have no neighbour or ring row.
The molecule field is ~molecules~. The interval is ~q1_ms~ and
~q3_ms~, or ~iqr_ms~ when that is the recorded interval. ~n~ is the
sample count. A row with no time, or a verdict of absent or DNF, is
written DNF.

For the classifier, polyhedral template matching, and IRA rows,
~ratio_vs_dseams~ is the ratio of medians. ~ratio_lo~ and ~ratio_hi~
give its interval.
