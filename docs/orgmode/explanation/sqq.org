#+TITLE: SQQ
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

SQQ 0.5.8 counts shortest-path chordless rings of size 4, 5 and 6.
d-SEAMS counts primitive rings, and that census includes
3-rings, which SQQ does not search. On a crystal the two censuses
are the same list. On a disordered hydrogen-bond network they are
not, and an ice label that reads those rings moves with them.

The ice comparison has three readings. The merged label puts
published CHILL+, which does not search rings, next to SQQ's ice
stage on chordless 6-rings. The primitive reading applies that same
SQQ ice rule to the d-SEAMS primitive 6-rings. The chordless reading
applies it to the d-SEAMS chordless 6-rings. The chordless reading
is the like-for-like label. The merged reading is the whole-tool
label.

* The 4096-molecule TIP4P/2005 frame

The oxygen-oxygen edges and the rings match.

| quantity | d-SEAMS | SQQ | agreement |

| 4-rings | 1758 | 1758 | 1 |

The merged ice label does not. Published CHILL+ names 4 molecules
ice and SQQ names 1129. The agreement is 0.724854. The SQQ rule on
the primitive 6-rings names 1075, agreement 0.986816 against SQQ's
1129. The same rule on the chordless 6-rings names 1129 on both
sides, agreement 1, all 4096 molecules.

The ring lists already match, so the merged gap is the choice of
ice rule. CHILL+ reads four bond correlations. SQQ reads the
chordless 6-rings. Once both sides use the chordless rings, the
labels agree.

Eight repeats, mean milliseconds. The ratio is SQQ divided by d-SEAMS.

SQQ is one Python process. Extra threads do not shorten it. The
d-SEAMS ring search does shorten, so the ratio rises from one
thread to eight.

* Where else the lists agree

Ideal ice Ic and ice Ih, at 4096 and at 110592 molecules, match on
the edges, the 4-rings, the 5-rings, the 6-rings, the chordless
6-rings and every ice reading. Each crystal has two 6-rings per
molecule and no 4-ring or 5-ring.

The 4096-molecule mW liquid matches on the edges and the rings.
Its merged ice agreement is 0.561768 (81 against 1851). The
chordless reading matches, 1851 on both sides.

The 250-molecule TIP4P oxygen frame matches on the edges and the
rings. Its merged ice agreement is 0.176000 (0 against 206). The
chordless reading matches, 206 on both sides.

The 110592-molecule mW liquid is the frame that misses. The
oxygen-oxygen edges are 259187 and 259186. The 4-rings are 34097
and 34098, the 5-rings 86218 and 86217, the 6-rings 85092 and
85094, and the chordless 6-rings 123915 and 123917. The chordless
ice reading is 50120 against 50121, agreement 0.999991. The merged
ice agreement is 0.561957.
