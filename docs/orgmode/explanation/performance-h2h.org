#+TITLE: Performance
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

Head-to-head times for ice Ic and a TIP4P/ice liquid at 884736
molecules. The widths are 1, 2, 4 and 8 threads. ~n~ is the sample
count on the row. Liquid rings-statistics-matter at one thread has
~n~ of 6. Every other row of this sitting has ~n~ of 8. The median
and the interquartile range are recomputed from the samples.

The figure is median time against thread count, ice and liquid side
by side. The vertical axis is logarithmic. It draws neighbours, rings,
\(q_6\) and CHILL+.

The rings rows are ~seams_frame_rings~ on the cutoff graph, depth 6:
the neighbour list and the search are one timed call. The last table
is a separate comparison of that full call, linkcell against the cell
list. ~n~ is 8 on each of those rows. The link, convert and search
stages of the same comparison are on
[[file:trace-threads.org][the thread controls]].

The release-candidate comparison, including cages and King's count, is
[[file:performance.org][the sitting page]].
Neighbour and ring rows for 4096 to 110592
molecules are on [[file:performance-range.org][the size range]].
How the comparisons differ is on
[[file:performance-overview.org][the overview]].
