#+TITLE: Ionic-liquid dumps
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

An ionic-liquid dump is a multi-type frame, and CHILL is the wrong
score for the ions.
Keep CHILL and cages on water with ~--water-types T~ (the neighbour
mask). ~--type~ is the load filter. Use ~seams rdf~ for a partial
g_IJ(r) under the dump minimum image. Use ~seams density-z~
for a type-resolved \(\rho(z)\) in a slit.

The bundled frame ~input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj~ has 12 atoms of
LAMMPS types 1, 2, 3 and 4 in a 24 Å cube. When ~--family~ is omitted
and the loaded frame has more than two types, stderr prints

#+begin_src text
hint: more than two LAMMPS types; set --family (default waterIce)
#+end_src

and the command still exits 0.

* Pick one type

~--type~ / ~-t~ selects the loaded type. ~0~, the default, keeps type
2 when that type is present, and type 1 when it is absent.
~density-z~ is the exception: ~0~ keeps every atom.
A dump whose species is not that guess needs the integer type.

#+begin_src bash
seams read input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj
seams read input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --type 3
#+end_src

The first command keeps type 2 and prints ~nop 4 frame 1 box 24 24 24~.
~--type 3~ keeps two atoms and prints ~nop 2 frame 1 box 24 24 24~.

* Unlike-type neighbours

~nneigh::neighListPair(rcut, cloud, typeI, typeJ)~ builds I-J
pairs. ~I == J~ is the like-type list (~neighListO~). Distances
use ~gen::periodicDistSq~, so a sheared dump box uses the same
MIC as the ice neighbour lists.

* Partial g(r)

#+begin_src bash
seams rdf input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --pair-types 1,2 --cutoff 6.0
seams rdf input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --pair-types 1,2 --cutoff 6.0 --bins 60
seams rdf input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --type 2 --cutoff 6.0
#+end_src

~--pair-types I,J~ selects the pair. The default is ~I = J = --type~,
a like-type g(r) of the guessed or requested species.
~--types~ is a deprecated exact alias of ~--pair-types~ and prints
~--types is deprecated; use --pair-types~.
~--cutoff~ is rmax. ~--bins~ is optional (default ~rmax / 0.1~, so
60 bins at 6 Å).

Stdout is ~# r g count~, then ~# types I J rmax R bins N volume V~,
then one ~r g count~ row per bin. The normalization volume is
~det(H)~ from the dump box. On this orthorhombic frame that volume
is 13824. Tilt ~xy xz yz~ enters H when the dump carries it. The
unlike-pair header is

#+begin_src text
# types 1 2 rmax 6 bins 60 volume 13824
#+end_src

~seams rdf~ is a pair histogram. CHILL and cages stay on water.

* Site kinds and ion vertices

LAMMPS ~type~ is a column, and the chemistry lives in a
~site::Table~ of type-to-kind (and optional atom-ID overrides).
~Table::of~ looks up the override first, then the type, else
~unspecified~. ~indicesOf(polar)~ is the union of ~cationHead~,
~anion~, and explicit ~polar~; ~indicesOf(apolar)~ is ~tail~ plus
explicit ~apolar~. ~site::ionCloud~ emits one vertex per ~molID~
that carries a cation head or anion: the unweighted COM of those
tagged atoms, unwrapped with ~gen::relDist~ to the first atom of
the group, with ~Point::type~ 1 (cation) or 2 (anion). A long-chain
cation COM sits on the polar head, because the tail atoms are not
in that average. Family is an input on the table. Types do not
set it.

On that ion cloud, ~nneigh::nearestUnlike(cloud, 1, 2)~ is each
ion's nearest unlike neighbour and the MIC distance.
~nneigh::mutualNearestUnlike~ keeps the mutual edges. Those
are contact pairs. ~seams cn~ is the coordination number, from
the integral of \(g(r)\).

* Polar and apolar domains

#+begin_src bash
seams domains input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --site '1=cationHead,2=anion,3=tail' --subset polar
seams domains input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --site '1=cationHead,2=anion,3=tail' --subset apolar
#+end_src

~seams domains~ builds the mask from ~site::indicesOf~ and walks
Stoddard's clusters on that subset.
Neighbours are the dump-MIC cutoff list (~--cutoff~, default
3.5 Å). Stdout is ~subset NAME n N largest L P~, and ~P = L / N~.
On this frame the lines are ~subset polar n 8 largest 4 P 0.5~ and
~subset apolar n 2 largest 1 P 0.5~. Missing ~--site~ prints
~domains needs --site~ and exits 2. ~--family~ applies to ice
scores. Domains ignore it.

* Confined layers

A slit or slab dump needs type-resolved \(\rho(z)\).

#+begin_src bash
seams density-z input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj
seams density-z input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --type 1 --bins 80
seams density-z input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --type 2 --axis z
#+end_src

~--type 0~ (default) histograms every atom. ~--type I~ keeps one
LAMMPS type. ~--axis~ is the Cartesian coordinate (~x~, ~y~, or
~z~; default ~z~). ~--bins~ is optional (default the bound span
over \(0.1\), 240 bins on this 24 Å cube).

Stdout is ~# z rho~ then one row per bin. The slice volume is
\(A_\perp \times dz\). \(A_\perp\) comes from dump H (~ly*lz~
for ~x~, ~lx*lz~ for ~y~, ~lx*ly~ for ~z~) using the recovered
edge lengths. The integral of ~rho * A * dz~ is the number of
selected atoms.

Confined IL work is this histogram plus the in-plane
~rdf2::rdf2Danalysis_AA~ sampler.
~seams chill input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --family confinedIL~ exits 2.

Molten salt is ~seams rdf~ / ~cn~ plus ~ionCloud~ when the melt
is multi-site. DES adds explicit donor H-bonds. Electrolyte is
ion--O rdf/cn plus water H-bonds. Silica / BeF2 is rdf/cn plus
Franzblau on the M--X graph and ~getq6~ on the tetrahedral
cation (~steinhardtQlVoronoi~ otherwise).

* Read the box

~seams read FILE~ prints ~nop N frame F box Lx Ly Lz~ and, when the
dump is triclinic (~box.size() >= 6~), the tilt ~xy xz yz~. On
~il_tiny.lammpstrj~ the default load prints ~nop 4 frame 1 box 24 24 24~.
Bound spans are the edge lengths on this orthorhombic frame. A sheared
dump carries tilt as well. See [[file:sheared-cells.org][Sheared cells]].

* Family names

~--family~ names the material. The default is ~waterIce~.
~seams chill~, ~chill-plus~, and ~cages~ exit 2 when ~--family~ is
~ionicLiquid~, ~moltenSalt~, ~des~, ~electrolyte~, ~confinedIL~,
~confinedWater~, or ~networkFormer~. The message names the family.
For ~ionicLiquid~ it is ~CHILL/TUM refused for family ionicLiquid~.
Unflagged ~seams chill~ stays the historical water path.

#+begin_src bash
seams rdf input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --pair-types 1,2 --cutoff 6.0
seams cn input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --pair-types 1,2 --cutoff 6.0
seams cn input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --ions --site '1=cationHead,2=anion'
seams pairs input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --site '1=cationHead,2=anion'
seams domains input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --site '1=cationHead,2=anion,3=tail' --subset polar
seams density-z input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --type 1 --bins 80
seams chill input/traj/il_tiny.lammpstrj --family ionicLiquid  # exits 2
#+end_src

The coordination line is ~# types 1 2 cutoff 6 cn 1.75 nI 4 nJ 4 volume 13824~.
The ion coordination line is ~cage ionCloud types 1 2 cutoff 3.5 degree 1.5 nCation 4 nAnion 4~.
The contact line is ~contact-pair count 4 nCation 4 nAnion 4~.
The last command exits 2.
