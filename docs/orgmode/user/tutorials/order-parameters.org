#+TITLE: Bond-orientational order parameters
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

The local Steinhardt parameter of atom \(i\) is

\[
q_l(i) = \sqrt{\frac{4\pi}{2l+1} \sum_{m=-l}^{l} |q_{lm}(i)|^2}
\]

with

\[
q_{lm}(i) = \frac{1}{N_b(i)} \sum_{j \in N(i)} Y_{lm}(\hat r_{ij}).
\]

\(N(i)\) is every neighbour inside the cutoff. The Lechner-Dellago
average replaces \(q_{lm}(i)\) with the mean of \(q_{lm}\) over \(i\)
and its neighbours, then uses the same sum.

The library computes degrees 3, 4, 6, 8, and 12. ~seams steinhardt~
prints the finite mean of each degree named by ~--l~. The default list
is 4 and 6. Degrees 4 and 6 use the solid-harmonic kernel on that
cutoff list. Degrees 3, 8, and 12 are computed when ~--l~ names them.

* One mixed frame

=input/traj/exampleTraj.lammpstrj= has 750 atoms. Type 2 is the 250
oxygens. Type 1 is hydrogen.

#+begin_src bash
seams read input/traj/exampleTraj.lammpstrj --type 2
#+end_src

#+begin_example
nop 250 frame 1 box 40 40 180.203
#+end_example

#+begin_src bash
seams steinhardt input/traj/exampleTraj.lammpstrj --type 2 --cutoff 3.5 --l 3
#+end_src

#+begin_example
nop 250 q3 0.374897 q3bar 0.187542
#+end_example

~q3~ is the mean of the finite local \(q_3\) values. ~q3bar~ is the
mean of the finite Lechner-Dellago values. On this frame those means
are 0.374897 and 0.187542.

#+begin_src bash
seams steinhardt input/traj/exampleTraj.lammpstrj --type 2 --cutoff 3.5 --l 3,4,6,8,12
#+end_src

#+begin_example
nop 250 q3 0.374897 q3bar 0.187542 q4 0.693844 q4bar 0.648011 q6 0.420854 q6bar 0.33063 q8 0.587703 q8bar 0.481713 q12 0.51294 q12bar 0.341973
#+end_example
