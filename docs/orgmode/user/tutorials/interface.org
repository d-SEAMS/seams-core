* Published CHILL+

The default list bonds every pair closer than ~--cutoff~. ~--layers~
bins cubic and hexagonal molecules, including reCubic and reHex, along
~--axis~. Axis 2 is \(z\).

#+begin_src bash
seams chill-plus input/traj/interface_f0.lammpstrj --type 1 --cutoff 3.5 --layers --axis 2
#+end_src

#+begin_example
nop 864 clathrate 3 cubic 1 hexagonal 352 interClathrate 25 interfacial 49 water 434
phi-c 0.00283286 stack HHHHHHHH....C.
#+end_example

phi-c is the cubic count divided by the cubic and hexagonal counts.
reCubic is counted with cubic, and reHex with hexagonal. This frame has
no reCubic or reHex molecules, so phi-c is 1/353, printed as 0.00283286.

The stack is one character per layer along \(z\). The 52.5093 Å edge is
split into 14 layers of equal width. H means the hexagonal count in
that layer exceeds the cubic count, C means the cubic count exceeds the
hexagonal count, and a dot means the layer has neither. The string is
eight H, four dots, one C, and a final dot.

* The ice front

The front uses a second binning: slabs of 3.7 Å from \(z = 0\). A
molecule is crystal when its label is cubic, hexagonal, reCubic, or
reHex. This frame has 353 crystal molecules.

From 7.4 Å through 14.8 Å every water in those slabs is crystal: 39,
then 72, then 69. The fraction then falls through one half.

| start (Å) | crystal | waters |
|-----------+---------+--------|
| 18.5 | 39 | 41 |
| 22.2 | 34 | 71 |
| 25.9 | 4 | 62 |
| 44.4 | 1 | 65 |

The crossing is at 22.035 Å, between the slab at 18.5 Å (39/41) and the
slab at 22.2 Å (34/71). Later slabs stay under one half. The slab at
44.4 Å holds the remaining crystal molecule.
