The build below used conda-forge GROMACS 2026.3, with the compiler and
Eigen already on the path. The same prefix also holds LAMMPS 2022.06.23,
OpenMM 8.6.0, and PLUMED 2.10.1.

#+begin_src bash
gmx-seams -f ice1h.gro -cutoff 3.5
#+end_src

The last frame prints:

#+begin_src text
gromacs hexagonal 128 six-rings 256 q6 0.625855
#+end_src

~engines/gromacs/run_mdrun.sh~ is a different path. It writes a short
frozen ~.tpr~, runs ~gmx mdrun -nsteps 4~, and then runs ~gmx-seams~ on
the ~.xtc~ that mdrun wrote. ~gmx-seams~ is post-processing after
~gmx mdrun~. It is a separate process, and it is not an in-process hook.
On the 64-molecule cubic cell the census is:

#+begin_src text
gromacs frames 5 ice 320 atoms 320
gromacs hexagonal 0 six-rings 128 q6 0.628519
#+end_src

~ice 320~ is the cubic total over 5 frames of 64 atoms.
~hexagonal~, ~six-rings~, and ~q6~ are the last frame.
~overhead_s~ is printed on the frames line and is machine-dependent.
One machine printed ~1.193596e-03~ and another printed ~8.988452e-04~.
The census lines stay the same.
