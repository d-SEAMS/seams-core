and measured times.

* OVITO

CHILL+ and the neighbour list against OVITO are rows of the
[[file:explanation/performance-h2h.org][performance tables]].

* freud

Per-atom \(q_4\), \(q_6\), their Lechner-Dellago averages, and their
means are compared on ideal ice Ih, ideal ice Ic, and a liquid frame.
All three codes use a strict 3.5 Å minimum-image cutoff. Values for
atoms with neighbours match pyscal3 to \(10^{-10}\). freud stores
\(q_l\) as float32, so crystals differ by a few units in the last
place and a disordered shell has a larger residual. An empty shell is
0 in d-SEAMS and NaN in freud and pyscal3.

The same cutoff \(q_6\) on the large sitting, and the published
literature numbers, are in the
[[file:explanation/performance-h2h.org][performance tables]] and
[[file:howto/literature-reproduce.org][the literature reproduction]].

* SQQ

SQQ ring times appear in the
[[file:explanation/performance-h2h.org][performance tables]] and the

[[file:explanation/sqq.org][SQQ page]] compares the chordless census

* PLUMED

The [[file:howto/engine-plumed.org][PLUMED engine page]] shows the
colvar. Its \(q_6\) timing appears in the
[[file:explanation/performance-h2h.org][performance tables]]. On the
liquid frame, the difference comes from PLUMED's rational switching
function rather than the hard cutoff. The
[[file:howto/literature-reproduce.org][literature reproduction]]
compares the same quantity with freud.

* ClavisCage TRACE

[[file:explanation/clavis-trace.org][ClavisCage TRACE]] is the cage
census and the serial TRACE timing against d-SEAMS at one and eight
threads.
