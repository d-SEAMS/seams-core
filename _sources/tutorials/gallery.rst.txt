===============
Science gallery
===============


The pictures are drawn when the book is built, from the same full runs
the slow tutorial tests check. OVITO's Python renderer colors the
atoms. When OVITO is missing, matplotlib draws the same coordinates
in 3D. The image files are produced in the build and are not stored
in the repository.

Each caption says what the colors mean and repeats the numbers the
tutorial test asserts. ``meson test docs_gallery`` builds the images
and checks those numbers against the tutorial reports.

.. include:: gallery-captions.rst

Molecule stills from chemparseplot sit below the OVITO gallery. They are
drawn by ``docs/scripts/render_molecules.py`` when the book is built.
