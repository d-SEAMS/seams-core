=========
Tutorials
=========


A tutorial is a learning path. You finish it with a skill, not a
lookup table. The one good tutorial on this site classifies the
in-tree mixed TIP4P dump two ways.

.. table::

    +-----------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
    | tutorial                                                  | you learn                                                  |
    +===========================================================+============================================================+
    | `Classify ice on the mixed TIP4P example <bulk-ice.rst>`_ | ``seams read``, CHILL+, cages, and why the scores disagree |
    +-----------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
    | `Ice and clathrate labels <ice-and-clathrate.rst>`_       | CHILL+ and cage signatures from Python                     |
    +-----------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
    | `Primitive rings <rings.rst>`_                            | Franzblau census on one frame                              |
    +-----------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
    | `Bond-orientational order <order-parameters.rst>`_        | ``q_l`` and the Lechner-Dellago average                    |
    +-----------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
    | `Confined water <confined-water.rst>`_                    | in-plane RDF, sheet rings, prisms                          |
    +-----------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
    | `Quasi-one-dimensional ice <nanotube.rst>`_               | where the nanotube and monolayer examples live             |
    +-----------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
    | `Paper figures <paper-figures.rst>`_                      | the in-tree numbers behind the 2020 figures                |
    +-----------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------+

How-tos solve a goal you already have. Reference names flags and
headers. Explanation is why the two classifiers exist.

The Python files under ``docs/tutorials`` are the runs. CI executes
them. The pages here say what you are doing and what number you
should see.
