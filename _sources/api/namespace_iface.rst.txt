.. index:: pair: function; formatQ6Growth
.. _doxid-namespaceiface_1acc0c4800681d1d5d1397be73a0cf9f77:

.. ref-code-block:: cpp
	:class: doxyrest-title-code-block

	std::string formatQ6Growth(const std::string& path, double dumpEveryPs, double cutoff = 3.5)

Lechner-Dellago q6 on the 3.5 A shell, the counting rule in Espinosa, Navarro, Sanz, Valeriani and Vega, J. Chem. Phys. 145, 211922 (2016), page 211922-3. The threshold is not fitted. The report is the fraction of molecules at or above each listed q6bar and local q6, plus the frame-1 histogram of the ice core and the liquid core.

	double gaussianStepWidth(double xi)

10-90 width of a step smoothed by a Gaussian of standard deviation xi. The profile is the normal cdf, so the width is 2 \* 1.2815515655446004 \* xi.

.. _doxid-namespaceiface_1a05f59884d0d3896c70075a635609406e:

.. ref-code-block:: cpp
	:class: doxyrest-title-code-block

	:ref:`WillardChandler<doxid-structiface_1_1_willard_chandler>` willardChandler(:ref:`molSys::PointCloud<doxid-structmol_sys_1_1_point_cloud>`<:ref:`molSys::Point<doxid-structmol_sys_1_1_point>`<double>, double> cloud, double xi = 2.4)

Instantaneous interface of Willard and Chandler, J. Phys. Chem. B 114, 1954 (2010). xi is their Gaussian width. The surface is the set of rays from the crystal center of mass on which the coarse density crosses the midpoint of the core and the outer liquid.
