Detailed Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

One rule set for classifying bond correlations :math:`c_{ij}`.

A bond is staggered when :math:`c_{ij} \le` staggeredMax, eclipsed when it falls in [eclipsedMin, eclipsedMax], and out of range otherwise. The CHILL and CHILL+ water rules are two instances; other hydrogen-bonded or tetrahedral materials register their own thresholds and coordination.
