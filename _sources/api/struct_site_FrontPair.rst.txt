Detailed Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Two planar fronts of one ice slab in a periodic box.

``:ref:`zEnter <doxid-structsite_1_1_front_pair_1a118ea488cb249bf52d8b2313631b2c6a>``` is where the ice fraction crosses ``cut`` on entering the ice along +z, and ``:ref:`zExit <doxid-structsite_1_1_front_pair_1a87e26df94c4679ae8eb8c97ba48cd02e>``` is the crossing on leaving it. ``:ref:`thickness <doxid-structsite_1_1_front_pair_1a5d86e67fc730eedaa46e9154418fe66c>``` is the ice run along z.
