.. index:: pair: variable; kQ6BarIceCut
.. _doxid-namespacenucleation_1af2f1477e5e5f99cbe98f4ab5b95091ae:

.. ref-code-block:: cpp
	:class: doxyrest-title-code-block

	double kQ6BarIceCut = 0.36

Lechner-Dellago :math:`\bar{q}_6` above this value is an ice-like environment. Set from the gap between a perfect mW Ic crystal and a liquid reference, not from the nucleation trajectory.

.. index:: pair: variable; kQ6SolidCutoffA
.. _doxid-namespacenucleation_1ae22c8a1c01ef593e140430321e5e0408:

.. ref-code-block:: cpp
	:class: doxyrest-title-code-block

	double kQ6SolidCutoffA = 3.2

q6 solid criterion of ten Wolde, Ruiz-Montero and Frenkel, J. Chem. Phys. 104, 9932 (1996), in the mW form of Haji-Akbari and Debenedetti, PNAS 112(34) (2015), doi:10.1073/pnas.1509267112, SI Table S1: neighbours within 3.2 A (the first minimum of the mW g(r)), and a molecule is solid when the mean over its neighbours of the normalised q6(i).q6\*(j) exceeds 0.5. The solid cluster is the largest component of solid molecules on the same 3.2 A graph. The Reinhardt chain exclusion is not applied.

	std::vector<double> q6DotScore(const :ref:`molSys::PointCloud<doxid-structmol_sys_1_1_point_cloud>`<:ref:`molSys::Point<doxid-structmol_sys_1_1_point>`<double>, double>& cloud, const std::vector<int>& offsets, const std::vector<int>& cols)

Per-molecule ten Wolde q6 score: the mean over neighbours within cutoff of ``Re q6(i).q6*(j) / (|q6(i)| |q6(j)|)``. A molecule without neighbours scores 0. The box is orthorhombic.
