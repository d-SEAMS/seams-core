Detailed Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Waters within ``halfWidth`` of either front, their primitive-ring census, and the coordination defects (a water whose oxygen neighbour count is not 4).

``:ref:`ringCensus <doxid-structsite_1_1_front_layer_report_1abdc4631419ba1a2fe7c9ceeb3d8cc545>`[s]`` is the number of primitive rings of size s that lie entirely in the layer. ``:ref:`coordination <doxid-structsite_1_1_front_layer_report_1ab05b6b54c5649c1ab5d92d56fcc5d113>`[c]`` counts waters with c neighbours, and the last bin also holds every larger count.
