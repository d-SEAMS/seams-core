====================
Performance overview
====================


Each median is printed on one page.

.. table::

    +--------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | page                                             | comparison                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
    +==================================================+===============================================================================================================================================================================================================================+
    | `Release-candidate sitting <performance.rst>`_   | Ideal ice Ic at 110592 and 884736 molecules, one thread and eight. ``n`` is 6. Median and type-7 interquartile range for neighbours, :math:`q_4`, :math:`q_6`, CHILL+, rings, cages, the row stored as PTM, and King's count. |
    +--------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | `Head-to-head <performance-h2h.rst>`_            | Ice Ic and a TIP4P/ice liquid at 884736 molecules, at 1, 2, 4 and 8 threads. ``n`` is 6 or 8. Median and interquartile range recomputed from the samples.                                                                     |
    +--------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | `4096 to 110592 <performance-range.rst>`_        | Neighbour and ring rows for 4096 to 110592 molecules, kept when ``n`` is at least 6, with the recorded interval.                                                                                                              |
    +--------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | `Signature thread controls <trace-threads.rst>`_ | The hydrate signature search, the eight kernels, and the cutoff ring call split into link, convert and search.                                                                                                                |
    +--------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

The operation string is the string the table stored. It is a different call on different pages.

On the release-candidate comparison, :math:`q_4` and :math:`q_6` are one Steinhardt call, and the rings row is ``seams_frame_rings`` on the cutoff graph: the neighbour list and the search together. The 884736-molecule rings row is that same call in the other comparison. The rings kernel on the thread-controls page is ``ringNetwork`` after the neighbour graph already exists. Those ring times are separate measurements.

The release-candidate PLUMED worker sets ``OMP_NUM_THREADS`` and does not set ``PLUMED_NUM_THREADS``. The head-to-head page holds the other PLUMED :math:`q_6` rows.
