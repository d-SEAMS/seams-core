============
Head-to-head
============


Head-to-head results that are already in this tree. Each link is the
page that records the numbers. The suite tests named here are the ones
that recompute them.

The summary table is written at build time from the CSVs under
``docs/data/comparisons/``. Those files are copies of the benchmark
commits named above the table. A timed row names the comparator
version, the thread count, the repeat count and the core count.

.. include:: explanation/comparisons-summary-tables.rst

OVITO
-----

`OVITO FindRings <explanation/ovito-findrings.rst>`_ is a subset of
the primitive-ring census. On K2,3, d-SEAMS reports three chordless
4-rings and FindRings reports two. On the 864-atom interface frame at
3.5 Å every d-SEAMS ring through size 10 is primitive, and each OVITO
bin is no larger than that census. ``meson test ovito_ring_counts`` and
the K2,3 case in ``tests/test_franzblau_extra.cpp`` recompute both.

OVITO 3.15 has no Steinhardt modifier. Local :math:`q_4` and :math:`q_6`, and
the Lechner-Dellago averages, are compared with freud and pyscal3
instead. ``meson test q4_q6_equal`` runs that comparison.

CHILL+ and the neighbour list against OVITO are rows of the
`performance tables <explanation/performance-h2h.rst>`_.

freud
-----

``meson test q4_q6_equal`` compares per-atom :math:`q_4` and :math:`q_6`, the
Lechner-Dellago averages, and the mean of each, on ideal ice Ih, ideal
ice Ic, and one liquid. The neighbour rule is the same in all three
codes: a pair counts when its minimum-image distance is strictly below
3.5 Å. pyscal3 matches to :math:`10^{-10}`. freud stores :math:`q_l` as
float32, so a crystal agrees to a few units in the last place and a
disordered shell keeps a larger residual. An empty shell is 0 in
d-SEAMS and NaN in freud and pyscal3. The
`q4 and q6 how-to <howto/q4-q6.rst>`_ records why that 0 stays:
the empty accumulator is the result, and a NaN switch would enter
every mean and every CHILL+ label.

The same cutoff :math:`q_6` on the large sitting, and the published
literature numbers, are in the
`performance tables <explanation/performance-h2h.rst>`_ and
`the literature reproduction <howto/literature-reproduce.rst>`_.

SQQ
---

SQQ ring times are a row of the
`performance tables <explanation/performance-h2h.rst>`_ and of
`the literature codes <howto/literature-h2h.rst>`_. The benchmark
records in the ``d-SEAMS/dseams2-benchmark-records`` dataset are the
source of those cells; ``docs/records/README.md`` names the revision.
The vendored SQQ census and the chordless and whole-tool ratios are on
`the SQQ page <explanation/sqq.rst>`_.

PLUMED
------

The colvar is `the PLUMED engine <howto/engine-plumed.rst>`_. Its
:math:`q_6` on the large sitting is a row of the
`performance tables <explanation/performance-h2h.rst>`_: on the
liquid the gap is the rational switch, not the hard cutoff. The
literature reproduction records the same comparison next to freud.
``meson test docs_engine_plumed`` runs the engine page.

ClavisCage TRACE
----------------

`ClavisCage TRACE <explanation/clavis-trace.rst>`_ is the cage
census and the serial TRACE timing against d-SEAMS at one and eight
threads. The cells are the vendored ClavisCage CSV, not a typed table.
