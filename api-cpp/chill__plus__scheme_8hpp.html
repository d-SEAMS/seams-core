Detailed Description

The two CHILL+ rules.

chill+ is the published rule. Table 1 footnote a of Nguyen and Molinero (J. Phys. Chem. B 2015, 119, 9369) counts neighbours inside the cutoff shell, and a crystal row requires that count to be 4. Equation 1 averages q_3 over the four nearest of those neighbours. The interfacial rows keep their neighbour test.

ovito-chill+ is the OVITO 3.17.2 Chill+ modifier: the same shell count, q_3 averaged over the whole cutoff shell, open c_ij edges, and no neighbour test.

Plain CHILL does not use this file.
