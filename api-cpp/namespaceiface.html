◆ formatQ6Growth()

std::string iface::formatQ6Growth ( const std::string & path,
double dumpEveryPs,
double cutoff = 3.5 )
nodiscard

Lechner-Dellago q6 on the 3.5 A shell, the counting rule in Espinosa, Navarro, Sanz, Valeriani and Vega, J. Chem. Phys. 145, 211922 (2016), page 211922-3. The threshold is not fitted. The report is the fraction of molecules at or above each listed q6bar and local q6, plus the frame-1 histogram of the ice core and the liquid core.

Instantaneous interface of Willard and Chandler, J. Phys. Chem. B 114, 1954 (2010). xi is their Gaussian width. The surface is the set of rays from the crystal center of mass on which the coarse density crosses the midpoint of the core and the outer liquid.
