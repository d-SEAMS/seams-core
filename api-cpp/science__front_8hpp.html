Detailed Description

Layer profiles and the single-trajectory critical size.

CHILL+ is the published Nguyen and Molinero rule (J. Phys. Chem. B 119, 9369, 2015): q3 over the four nearest neighbours, a crystal row of four neighbours inside the cutoff, and the interfacial neighbour test in Table 1. The OVITO rule is applied separately and is not used to choose these labels. The shell label is the second-shell template: 0 hexagonal, 1 cubic, 2 stacking-disordered, and -1 otherwise. q6-bar is the Lechner-Dellago average on the same 3.5 A cutoff. None of these thresholds is fitted.

The critical size on one spontaneous trajectory is the mean size of the largest cluster in the contiguous band around its minimum. The band stops where the size exceeds that minimum by the larger of 5 molecules and 15 percent. The interval is a percentile bootstrap of that mean. A minimum in the first two frames, followed by a larger cluster, is an upper bound: the dump does not contain a smaller nucleus. It is not a committor.
