Detailed Description

The two published CHILL+ rules on an interface, and the ring labels of confined ice.

Thresholds are the 2015 values. Nothing here is fitted.

Nguyen and Molinero, J. Phys. Chem. B 119 (2015) 9369, equation 1 and Table 1: q3 is the mean of Y_3m over the four nearest neighbours, a staggered bond is c(i,j) <= -0.8, an eclipsed bond is -0.35 <= c(i,j) <= 0.25, a crystal row has four neighbours inside the cutoff, and an interfacial row keeps the neighbour test in footnote a of that table.

OVITO 3.17.2 Chill+ (manual page chill_plus) and the mdapy kernel that was cross-checked against OVITO's ChillPlusModifier: q3 runs over every neighbour inside the cutoff, a staggered bond is c(i,j) < -0.8, an eclipsed bond is -0.35 < c(i,j) < 0.25, and an interfacial row is two staggered bonds, or three staggered and no eclipsed bond, with no neighbour test.
