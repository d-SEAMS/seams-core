29// First-shell prefilter. It is not the crystal decision: that is the
30// second-shell fingerprint and the scale-free fit. On TIP4P/2005 at its
31// melting point a quench-majority crystal has tetrahedral-order 5th
32// percentile 0.87 and the liquid median is 0.80, so 0.85 lets the thermal
33// crystal reach the second shell and still sits above the liquid median.
34// 0.94 is the floor of a quenched mW crystal and cuts that thermal body.
35inline constexpr double kThermalQ = 0.85;
36// Ratio of the longest to the shortest of the four nearest bonds. The
37// same crystal's 95th percentile is 1.17.
38inline constexpr double kThermalBondRatio = 1.20;
39
40// xyz is n by 3, row major. lat rows are the cell edges. kind[i] is written
41// as ice Ih (0), ice Ic (1), ice Isd (2), or -1.
42// x, y, z are length n. Column-major coordinates, no interleaving copy.
43// openFace completes a short second shell on a bonded neighbour that has
44// fewer than four bonds. A full coordination shell is never padded. Off
45// by default, which keeps the labels the shell match gave before.
