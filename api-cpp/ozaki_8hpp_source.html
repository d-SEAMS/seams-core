4// Emulated products for the short dots and the one tiny GEMM in d-SEAMS.
5//
6// Ozaki scheme I splits a floating-point vector into slices whose products
7// are exact in FP64 (Ozaki, Ogita and Oishi, Numer. Algorithms 59, 95, 2012).
8// Ozaki scheme II replaces that slicing by an integer matrix product modulo
9// several coprime moduli and a Chinese-remainder reconstruction (Ozaki,
10// Uchino and Imamura, arXiv:2504.08009, 2025). The INT8 products use
11// AVX-512 VNNI or AMX-INT8 when the shape fills a tile; shorter products
12// use an INT32 accumulator, which is exact for the lengths that occur here.
13//
14// TwoProd / TwoSum and pairwise summation are the error-free transformations
15// of Dekker, Knuth, and Ogita, Rump and Oishi (SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 26,
16// 2005). Pairwise summation is the reduction whose error Higham bounds by
17// gamma_{ceil(log2 n)} (Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms,
18// SIAM, 2002). It does not depend on a left-to-right fold.
