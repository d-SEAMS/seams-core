CHILL+ definition.

PUBLISHED averages q_lm over the four closest neighbours (Nguyen and Molinero, equation 1) and uses inclusive edges. OVITO averages q_lm over every neighbour inside the cutoff, the Chill+ modifier's 3.5 A shell, with that modifier's open edges.
